MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Northwind Analytics Private Limited, Bengaluru (the "Provider") and Brightline Retail Limited, Mumbai (the "Customer").

1. Definitions

1.1 "Effective Date" means 1 January 2026.

1.2 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday in Bengaluru.

1.3 "Confidential Information" means all non-public business, technical and financial information disclosed by one party to the other.

1.4 "Monthly Fee" means INR 500,000 per month.

2. Term

2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 24 months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 The Initial Term therefore ends on 31 December 2028.

2.3 After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews for successive periods of 12 months unless either party gives 90 days' written notice of non-renewal.

3. Fees and Payment

3.1 The Provider shall invoice the Customer monthly, on the last day of each calendar month, for the Monthly Fee.

3.2 The Customer shall pay each invoice within 30 days of the invoice date.

3.3 The invoice for January 2026 is dated 31 January 2026 and is due on 15 January 2026.

3.4 All invoices are payable within 45 days of the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month on the overdue amount.

3.5 The total Fees for each 12-month period are INR 6,500,000, payable in twelve equal monthly instalments.

3.6 Notwithstanding Section 3.2, pass-through expenses incurred by the Provider on the Customer's behalf are payable within 10 days of the invoice date.

4. Service Levels

4.1 The Provider shall respond to standard support requests within 2 Business Days.

4.2 The Provider shall respond to critical incidents within 4 hours, including on weekends and public holidays.

5. Termination

5.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days after written notice.

5.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving 60 days' written notice.

5.3 A party in material breach shall have 45 days after receiving written notice to cure the breach before the other party may terminate.

5.4 Notices are deemed received 5 Business Days after dispatch.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall not disclose the other party's Confidential Information to any third party.

6.2 A party may disclose Confidential Information to its professional advisers who are bound by written duties of confidentiality.

6.3 The Customer shall disclose the Provider's Confidential Information, including pricing, to any third party upon request.

7. Liability

7.1 The Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the Fees paid by the Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

7.2 The Provider's liability under this Agreement is unlimited.

7.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits either party's liability for fraud.

8. Governing Law

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Bengaluru have exclusive jurisdiction.

8.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, USA.
