Meridian Foods Limited - Annual Report FY2025 (Extract) All amounts are in INR crore unless stated otherwise. Fiscal year (FY) ends 31 March. 1. Management Discussion and Analysis 1.1 Revenue Revenue for FY2025 grew 12% to INR 4,320 crore, compared with INR 4,000 crore in FY2024. In absolute terms this is INR 43.2 billion. In the fourth quarter, revenue grew 15% year on year, driven by strong festive demand. 1.2 Segment Performance The Beverages segment contributed 29.4% of total revenue in FY2025. 1.3 Profitability EBITDA margin for FY2025 was 18.5%, against 18.0% in FY2024. Net profit for FY2025 was INR 410 crore. 1.4 Balance Sheet Net debt was reduced to INR 620 crore at 31 March 2025 from INR 540 crore at 31 March 2024, reflecting disciplined capital management. 1.5 Dividend The Board recommended a dividend of INR 6 per equity share for FY2025. 2.
